Capital (assessment) vs Cash + Home
Capital (assessment) is smooth wealth (cash + home) used to decide funding: it changes by weekly income minus the weekly fee. Cash and Home show the actual cash flow, where care-home fees are billed in N-week lumps paid upfront, so they step down each billing period.
Assumptions
Income is counted in full and tax is ignored. The home is assumed unoccupied, so it counts as capital. Treat the result as a planning model, not formal advice.
