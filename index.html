Notes

Chart lines
Cash and Home show the precise values, where care home fees are billed in regular lumps paid upfront, with any prepay weeks up front and a deposit that is held until the end and then returned.
Capital (assessment) is smoothed wealth (cash + home) used to decide subsidies.
Assumptions
Income is counted in full and tax is ignored.
The home is assumed unoccupied, so it counts as capital.
Treat the result as a planning model, not formal advice.
